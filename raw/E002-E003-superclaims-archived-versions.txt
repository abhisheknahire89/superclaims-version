E002 — snapshot 2026-01-20 (https://web.archive.org/web/20260120212003/https://superclaims.ai/)
- Headline claimed "10x Faster & 3x Cheaper"; hero metrics: 5 min TAT, 70% cost reduction, 99% accuracy.
- Framed the product as a "digital adjudication team" of autonomous agents that "reads, reasons, and decides".
- Four named agents:
  * Pre-Authorization Agent — interprets pre-auth documents, validates eligibility/coverage, recommends "Approve, Query, or Reject".
  * Non-Medico Validation Agent — item-level scrutiny for non-payables, tariff mismatches, policy violations.
  * Medico Review Agent — reads medical narratives and diagnosis codes; checks necessity and admission validity.
  * Audit & Compliance Agent — validates other agents' outputs, detects anomalies/fraud indicators, builds audit trails.
- Impact tiles: 70% cost reduction; "90min → 5min TAT"; "99% operational precision"; "100k+ claims/month"; improved loss ratios.
- Claimed "Proprietary adjudication protocols, curated clinical datasets" as a moat.
- Three-step flow (Ingest, Reason, Settle) under a heading promising "zero manual steps"; the Settle step said
  "payment triggers fired automatically".
- Footer © 2025.

E003 — snapshot 2026-06-16 (https://web.archive.org/web/20260616151934/https://superclaims.ai/)
- Same "10x Faster & 3x Cheaper" headline; described the platform as automating "the end-to-end claims review process"; states IPD and OPD support.
- Impact tiles now: ~10 mins (from 300+ minutes); >97% "Decision precision for accurate payouts"; ~70% cost reduction; 100K+ claims monthly.
- Agents renamed: Non-Medico Agent (administrative evaluation), Medico Agent (clinical review), FWA Agent (anomaly detection),
  Audit Agent (decision and logging).
- Seven numbered validations: hospital tariff, SOC, policy benefit plan, NME identification, deduction calculations,
  detailed medical scrutiny, FWA detection. "100+ structured attributes".
- Integration via API, TMS integration or secure portal. Footer © 2026.
