Structure of the page (in order) and what each section says
1. Banner: the site states Superclaims is now part of ekincare (link to ekincare.com).
2. Hero: headline about adjudicating health claims "in minutes, not hours"; positions the product as AI that converts
   unstructured claim documents into consistent, audit-ready decisions for insurers and TPAs, claiming up to 70% lower operating cost.
3. Product overview: an "AI-powered claim adjudication engine for insurers and TPAs"; states support for "both IPD and OPD claim adjudication".
   Three capability cards: structuring claim data (field extraction, bill line categorisation); applying review rules
   (policy, tariff, clinical, expense, fraud checks "against the configured workflow"); preparing the decision
   (deductions, findings, reasoning "in a traceable package for reviewers").
4. Operational impact, with the caveat that results vary by claim type, document quality, configuration and review policy:
   ~10 mins typical processing time (down from 300+ minutes); >97% "decision precision across evaluated claims";
   ~70% operational cost reduction "in deployed workflows"; 100K+ claims processed monthly "across active deployments".
5. Four stages handled by "specialized agents": intake and structure; financial checks; clinical and anomaly review;
   decision and audit trail ("for reviewer validation"); the stage output is labelled "Review-ready decision package".
   A sub-heading near the top reads "From claim documents to review-ready decisions."
6. Review criteria: "extracts 100+ structured attributes"; checks listed as hospital tariff validation, Schedule of Charges (SOC)
   checks, policy benefit plan validation, deduction calculations, detailed medical scrutiny, Non-Medical Expenses (NME)
   identification, Fraud/Waste/Abuse (FWA) detection, traceable findings and deductions.
7. Security: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified ISMS; scope described as software development and support for end-to-end health claims;
   certificate 146637/A/0001/UK/En valid through 13 April 2029; issued to Cardit Labs Private Limited, "the legal entity for Superclaims".
8. Integration routes: API; TMS integration; secure portal (for when direct integration "is not yet practical").
9. Call to action: 30-minute live demo using "representative claim data", booked via Cal.com.
10. Footer: © 2025; "An ekincare company"; links to LinkedIn, Security, Privacy, Terms.
