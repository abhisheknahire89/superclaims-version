- **Regulatory excerpts** (E-REG-*). Verbatim passages from official IRDAI documents on irdai.gov.in, read on 9 Oct 2026, with document references. These are excerpts only. The full documents are linked in the ledger.
- **Screenshots** (E004). The certificate image, with the certification body signatory's name and signature redacted.
- **research-state.json**. The browsing log in the product-reverse `state.json` format.
- **Personal data.** No viewer, employee or patient data is recorded. All claim data elsewhere in the pack is synthetic.
