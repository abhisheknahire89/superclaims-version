# ADR-18 — Local reviewer operations and access management

Accepted 12 October 2026. The assignment forbids external delivery/integrations. Approved query letters are downloadable local PDF/JSON; SENT means available in the workbench, never emailed or delivered to a hospital. Unapproved drafts cannot be exported. Replies must link an active same-claim document and a still-open item; they mark the evaluation stale and reprocess, never automatically resolve a material conflict. Overdue reminders record a local audit event; they cannot close a claim. Four-eyes decisions and insurer-only CRC boundaries remain in the existing decision services.

Supervisor assignment requires an active same-tenant reviewer, current ETag and open claim. Queues filter status/workflow/assignment/deadline/reference, preserve masked names, and page through due-sorted claims. Claim exports mask identity; CSV formula-like values are escaped. Audit exports require supervisor/auditor. Metrics show measured counts and a freshness timestamp; no external delivery, clinical efficacy or real-world cost projection is implied.

Tenant admins manage local roles/limits/activation/password resets with password confirmation, serialized per-tenant changes, retained role history, immediate session revocation and an append-only RLS administration ledger (0006). Self-removal of administrator access and removing the last active supervisor are refused. Clinical resolution is restricted to MEDICAL_OFFICER: the older implementation incorrectly allowed a supervisor; 03 section4 only permits supervisor nonclinical resolutions. Closed claims cannot accept reviewer mutations without reopen; stale evaluation targets are rejected.

Proof: test_operations covers assignment/metrics/export permissions, TC-QRY-01, evidence reply/reprocessing, nonresponse staying open, user creation, last-supervisor protection and negative admin-ledger RLS. Browser evidence and full-suite results are recorded in PROJECT_CONTEXT at milestone close.
