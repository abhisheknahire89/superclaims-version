# ADR-16 — Local cashless and OPD workflows

Accepted12October2026. The complete-product assignment brings the older P2 cashless/OPD scope into the local product. No TMS adapter or hosted service is added. PREAUTH and ENHANCEMENT use source-grounded itemised estimates and request documents; cashless discharge requires a final bill and discharge summary. OPD uses an itemised bill, prescription and receipt, with configured benefit limits. These checklists are blocking for the newly introduced workflows. Estimates are provisional authorisations, never final bill settlement.

Each cashless stage is a distinct claim with explicit parent and root references. Enhancement requires an approved same-tenant/policy/member/hospital parent; one active successor prevents branches. Enhancement amounts are cumulative and cannot fall below the preceding authorisation. On each human decision, the policy-member row lock protects an append-only release of the prior group's reservation and reservation of the new cumulative amount. Discharge may reduce the final amount. Parent stages cannot be reopened after continuation. Existing SI exact-money traces remain unchanged. A paid external opening ledger remains distinct from these reservations.

OPD benefit caps apply after normal admissibility/co-pay/SI and before rounding, cite policy authority and bill evidence, and are rechecked under the SI row lock. No configured OPD benefit means abstention. No automatic approval/denial is introduced. Workflow clocks use1hour preauth, configured synthetic enhancement,3hour discharge and15day synthetic reimbursement settings. Queries do not pause clocks or close cases automatically.

State transitions are checked at ingestion, evaluation completion and decision boundaries. Reopening releases only the net outstanding reservation (old code released every historic reservation again); prior-decision count forces a new evaluation so an earlier final decision cannot be reused. Closed claims ignore late background evaluations. Shared authentication/RBAC and insurer-vs-TPA boundaries are unchanged in this milestone.

Proof: tests/test_workflows covers the preauth→enhancement→discharge chain, exact net reservation, OPD cap, invalid parent/state and deadlines. workflow_ui exercises four seeded reviews and human decisions plus linked enhancement registration. These are synthetic product tests, not clinical criteria validation or insurer integration.
